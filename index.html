Official Team History & Documentation

Origins (September 29)

The bot was originally created on September 29 as a singing bot by a single developer named AngelGTP. After its initial creation, the project was left inactive for a short time.

Rebranding & Creation of the Team (October 28)

On October 28, the project was officially revived and renamed to the Republic of Chile, marking the true creation of our team identity.

Logo Evolution

When the bot became the Republic of Chile, we updated our visual identity. We transitioned away from our original Chile Countryball logo and adopted the official flag of Chile.

Development Platforms

We initially built and hosted our bot using BotGhost. However, after running into paywalls and premium feature restrictions that locked us out of essential tools, we migrated our development over to Kite.
